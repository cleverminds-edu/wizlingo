# WizLingo Comprehensive Curriculum Framework
## Nursery to Grade 12 English Language Learning

---

## 1. CURRICULUM OVERVIEW

### Age-Grade Mapping
```
Nursery (N)     : 3-4 years   | CEFR Level: Pre-A1 (Foundation)
Kindergarten(K) : 4-5 years   | CEFR Level: Pre-A1 (Foundation)
Grade 1-2       : 6-8 years   | CEFR Level: A1 (Beginner)
Grade 3-4       : 8-10 years  | CEFR Level: A1-A2 (Elementary)
Grade 5-6       : 10-12 years | CEFR Level: A2 (Pre-Intermediate)
Grade 7-8       : 12-14 years | CEFR Level: A2-B1 (Intermediate)
Grade 9-10      : 14-16 years | CEFR Level: B1 (Upper-Intermediate)
Grade 11-12     : 16-18 years | CEFR Level: B1-B2 (Advanced)
```

### Curriculum Philosophy
- **Communicative Approach**: Real communication over grammar drills
- **Task-Based Learning**: Students solve real problems using English
- **Thematic Organization**: Content organized around meaningful themes
- **Spiral Curriculum**: Revisit concepts at increasing complexity
- **Culturally Relevant**: Include Indian context and global perspectives
- **Skill Integration**: LSRW (Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing) integrated
- **Scaffolded Learning**: Clear progression from simple to complex

## 2. DIFFICULTY LEVELS FRAMEWORK

### Level Progression Across K12

```
NURSERY & KG (Pre-A1: Foundation)
├─ Level 1: Phonemic Awareness
│  └─ Single sounds, sound recognition, listening discrimination
├─ Level 2: Basic Recognition
│  └─ Word recognition, picture-word matching, simple commands
└─ Level 3: Early Production
   └─ Simple words, 1-2 word utterances, basic responses

GRADE 1-2 (A1: Beginner)
├─ Level 4: Emerging Production
│  └─ 2-4 word phrases, common vocabulary, familiar topics
├─ Level 5: Developing Fluency
│  └─ Simple sentences, present tense, personal information
└─ Level 6: Expanded Production
   └─ Connected sentences, basic past/future, short stories

GRADE 3-4 (A1-A2: Elementary)
├─ Level 7: Intermediate Fluency
│  └─ Multi-clause sentences, descriptive language, varied tenses
├─ Level 8: Advanced Elementary
│  └─ Complex sentences, past tense, narrative skills
└─ Level 9: Pre-Intermediate Production
   └─ Varied sentence structures, modal verbs, intermediate comprehension

GRADE 5-6 (A2: Pre-Intermediate)
├─ Level 10: Intermediate Comprehension
│  └─ Abstract concepts, detailed texts, inference skills
├─ Level 11: Intermediate Production
│  └─ Varied vocabulary, conditional structures, persuasive writing
└─ Level 12: Upper-Intermediate Emerging
   └─ Complex narratives, informal writing, presentation skills

GRADE 7-8 (A2-B1: Intermediate)
├─ Level 13: Strong Intermediate
│  └─ Academic language, subjunctive mood, analytical writing
├─ Level 14: Upper-Intermediate
│  └─ Critical thinking, debate skills, formal writing
└─ Level 15: B1 Lower (Upper-Intermediate)
   └─ Spontaneous expression, abstract discussion, essay writing

GRADE 9-10 (B1: Upper-Intermediate)
├─ Level 16: B1 Upper
│  └─ Complex arguments, nuanced expression, literary analysis
├─ Level 17: B1-B2 Bridge
│  └─ Idiomatic language, sophisticated writing, research skills
└─ Level 18: Near B2
   └─ Native-like fluency, specialized vocabulary, advanced writing

GRADE 11-12 (B1-B2: Advanced)
├─ Level 19: B2 Lower (Advanced)
│  └─ Nuanced expression, academic discourse, creative writing
└─ Level 20: B2 Upper (Mastery)
   └─ Near-native proficiency, specialized topics, literature analysis
```

### Difficulty Indicators Per Level

| Level | Vocabulary | Sentence Structure | Text Length | Topics | Cognitive Load |
|-------|------------|-------------------|------------|--------|-----------------|
| 1-3 | 50-100 words | 1 word, simple 2-word | Single word | Familiar | Very Low |
| 4-6 | 100-300 words | 2-4 words, simple phrases | 5-10 words | Daily life | Low |
| 7-9 | 300-800 words | Simple sentences | 1-2 sentences | School activities | Moderate |
| 10-12 | 800-1500 words | Complex sentences | 2-3 paragraphs | Academic topics | Moderate-High |
| 13-15 | 1500-2500 words | Multi-clause, varied structures | 3-5 paragraphs | Abstract concepts | High |
| 16-18 | 2500-4000 words | Sophisticated structures | Article/essay | Specialized topics | Very High |
| 19-20 | 4000+ words | Native-like complexity | Long-form | Academic/literary | Maximum |

```
│  └─ Objectives: Sensory awareness, description words
│  └─ Vocabulary: see, hear, touch, smell, taste
```

## 5. CONTENT GENERATION TEMPLATES

### Template 1: Reading Passage Generator

```yaml
TEMPLATE: ReadingPassageGenerator

INPUTS:
  - theme: string                    # e.g., "Animals", "Family", "School"
  - grade: string                    # e.g., "Grade1", "Grade5"
  - difficulty_level: integer        # 1-20
  - length: string                   # "short" (50-100), "medium" (100-200), "long" (200-400)
  - topics_focus: list[string]       # e.g., ["vocabulary", "grammar", "cultural"]
  - reading_level: string            # "narrative", "informational", "descriptive"

PROMPT_TEMPLATE: |
  You are an expert language curriculum designer. Generate an engaging English reading passage for {grade} students.
  
  Requirements:
  - Theme: {theme}
  - Reading Level: {reading_level}
  - Target Length: {length} words
  - Target Difficulty (1-20): {difficulty_level}
  - Pedagogical Focus: {topics_focus}
  - Include {vocabulary_count} target vocabulary words from the unit
  - Use age-appropriate content and scenarios
  - Include cultural relevance for Indian context where appropriate
  - Create engaging characters and situations
  - End with a clear conclusion
  
  Format:
  {{
    "title": "...",
    "passage": "...",
    "vocabulary_words": ["word1", "word2", ...],
    "comprehension_questions": [
      {{
        "id": "q1",
        "question": "...",
        "type": "multiple_choice",
        "options": ["A", "B", "C", "D"],
        "correct_answer": "A",
        "difficulty": "easy"
      }},
      ...
    ],
    "word_count": number,
    "reading_time_minutes": number,
    "cultural_elements": ["..."],
    "moral_lesson": "..."
  }}

OUTPUT_STRUCTURE:
  - Title: Engaging, theme-related
  - Passage: 50-400 words depending on difficulty
```
