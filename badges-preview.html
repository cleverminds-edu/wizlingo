🎨 Modern Design Principles

This redesign focuses on simplicity, clarity, and shareability - perfect for social media and mobile screens.

✓ Minimal & Clean Removed ornate elements. Clear visual hierarchy with day number as hero.
✓ Color-Coded Each badge has a unique accent color for instant recognition and visual appeal.
✓ Mobile-First Optimized for small screens. Works perfectly as a screenshot for social sharing.
✓ Shareable Clean card design is perfect for sharing on WhatsApp, Instagram, etc.
✓ Clear Status Locked/Unlocked clearly indicated with icon and color. Progress visible at a glance.
✓ Modern Typography Bold, contemporary fonts. Strong hierarchy between day, name, and emoji.
✓ Day-Based System Focus on consistent daily practice. Users see their day milestone prominently.
✓ Progress Tracking Visible progress bars for locked badges keep users motivated.

Key Improvements: Removed competing visual elements. Simplified color palette. Better contrast. Emoji integrated naturally. Status clear at first glance. Perfect for mobile screenshots and social sharing. Modern, professional appearance that feels premium without being over-designed.
